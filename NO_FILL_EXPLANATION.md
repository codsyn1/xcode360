### **1. New AdMob Account (Most Common)**

- **Reason**: Ad inventory needs time to populate
- **Solution**: Wait for approval process

- **Issue**: AdMob doesn't know about your app
- **Solution**: Add app manually in AdMob dashboard

### **3. Payment Info Missing**
- **Issue**: No payment method on file
- **Solution**: Add payment details in AdMob settings

- **Issue**: Limited ads in your region
- **Solution**: Try different times/locations
