XCODE360 Account Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data

How to Request Account Deletion

To request the deletion of your XCODE360 account, please send an email to our support team with the following information:

Required Information:

  • Your registered email address
  • Clear request to delete your account and all associated data
  • Confirmation that you understand this action is permanent
  • Any specific reason for deletion (optional)

Contact Information

Email: jehangir.business@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - XCODE360

Expected Response Time: Within 7 business days

What Happens After Deletion

⚠️ Important Notice

Once your account is deleted, the following actions will occur:

  • Your account will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered
  • All personal data associated with your account will be removed
  • You will lose access to all app features and saved information
  • Any subscriptions or premium features will be cancelled
  • This action is irreversible

Data Deletion Timeline

After we receive your deletion request:

  • We will verify your identity and account ownership
  • Account deletion will be processed within 7 business days
  • You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete
  • Most data will be immediately removed, some backup data may be deleted within 30 days
